# Product package contents

Core cards describe the capabilities available for an agreed product plan. The proposal confirms selected services and deliverables. Solution add-ons are optional and assessed separately.

## GrowthOS

A digital marketing foundation, with practical automation options when enquiries need a clearer next step. Explore the service mix and the solutions we can add to your plan.

Core services and setup:
- Website Design & Development
- SEO & Search Visibility
- Local SEO & Google Business Profile
- Content & Social Media Marketing
- Paid Advertising & Landing Pages
- Analytics & Growth Review

Optional solutions:
- Lead Capture & Starter CRM: Connect a website form or landing-page enquiry to a customer record, a named owner and a simple follow-up stage. HighLevel or a suitable existing CRM can be assessed for the task.
- WhatsApp Automation: Add approved enquiry acknowledgements, useful next-step messages or follow-up reminders through the agreed WhatsApp setup. Templates, channel permissions and provider availability are checked during scoping.
- Email & Appointment Follow-up: Connect a relevant email sequence or booking request to the next action your team needs to take. The scope can include confirmations and reminders, using the email and calendar tools agreed for the business.
- AI-assisted Enquiry Automation: Use approved business information to help prepare a simple reply, summarise an enquiry or route a request for review. The task has a defined boundary and a clear handover to your team.

Advertising spend, messaging usage, third-party software and optional solution work are priced separately where relevant. The proposal confirms the selected channels and scope.

## BusinessOS

The customer workspace, communication tools and workflow services behind a more connected workday. Explore the core setup and the additional solutions for your operating needs.

Core services and setup:
- CRM & Customer Workspace
- Omnichannel Conversations
- Calling & Appointments
- Support & Knowledge
- Workflow Automation
- Reporting & Team Visibility

Optional solutions:
- AI Conversation & Calling Workflows: Add a defined AI-assisted chat or voice task, such as answering an approved service question or collecting booking context. Escalation and handover remain part of the workflow.
- Advanced App & Data Connections: Assess additional connections between the CRM and the business applications your team uses. Available APIs, data mapping, permissions and failure handling determine what can be implemented.
- Payment-event & Invoice Follow-up: Connect supported payment or invoice events to approved customer records, notifications and follow-up tasks. Financial approvals and the source accounting system keep their agreed responsibilities.
- Customer Re-engagement Automation: Build a relevant email or WhatsApp follow-up around customer stages, service milestones or an agreed campaign. Audience permissions, message content and response ownership are confirmed first.

HighLevel and other software subscriptions, communication usage and custom integration work depend on the selected scope. The tool marquee shows implementation options, rather than a mandatory bundle.

## AgenticOS

AI-agent development, integration and evaluation services for a defined business task. Explore the core build and the channels or knowledge connections that can extend it.

Core services and setup:
- AI Use-case & Workflow Design
- Specialist AI Agents
- Approved Tools & Integrations
- Human Approval & Escalation
- Testing & Evaluation
- Monitoring & Refinement

Optional solutions:
- CRM & Service-desk Connections: Connect the approved agent task with customer context or support requests from an agreed system. Read access, draft preparation and record changes receive separate permissions and review rules.
- WhatsApp & Web Chat Interfaces: Provide a customer-facing entry point for an agreed agent task through supported messaging or web chat. The conversation uses approved information and retains a practical path to a person.
- Voice-agent Workflows: Assess an AI-assisted voice task for a defined enquiry, routing or appointment process. Supported telephony, language needs, recording arrangements and escalation are reviewed before implementation.
- Knowledge-base & Document Retrieval: Add an agreed collection of business documents or approved answers to support retrieval and draft preparation. Source quality, access boundaries and review responsibility are part of the setup.

Model/API usage, hosting, provider subscriptions and additional integrations are scoped separately. Tool access, evaluation and approval responsibilities are recorded for the implementation.

## AI Marketing Platforms

Content, creative production and platform services brought into one reviewable marketing workflow. Explore the core workspace and the optional connections around it.

Core services and setup:
- Brand & Campaign Briefs
- AI Copywriting & Content Drafts
- AI-assisted Visual & Video Production
- Campaign Planning & Content Calendars
- Brand Review & Approval
- Tool Setup & Custom Workspace

Optional solutions:
- SEO & Content Workflow Connections: Connect relevant keyword and content briefs with draft preparation and editorial checks. Approved website publishing can be assessed as a separate connection to the chosen content platform.
- Social Publishing & Scheduling: Assess supported social-platform connections for approved posts and a practical publishing schedule. Your team retains responsibility for final content approval and account access.
- CRM, Email & Campaign Handovers: Connect an approved marketing asset or campaign brief with the team and system responsible for using it. Email setup and CRM actions depend on the provider, audience permissions and scope.
- Custom Creative Automations: Connect a recurring creative task, such as turning an approved brief into draft variations or routing assets for review. Templates, tools and quality checks are agreed before automating the sequence.

Creative-tool licences, model usage, paid media and custom connections are separate where relevant. The scope identifies formats, production tasks, review stages and ongoing support.

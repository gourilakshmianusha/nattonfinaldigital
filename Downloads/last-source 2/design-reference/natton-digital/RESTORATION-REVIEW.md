# Company pages, Resources and video heroes

Restores the original Resources menu's four entries in desktop navigation, mobile navigation, footer and page search: Free Growth Tools, AI Readiness Assessment, ROI & Automation Calculator, and Blogs. About Us now opens a dedicated page, with the original company story, mission, vision, offerings and partnership steps adapted to the current design. Contact, consultation, Resources hub, comparison, delivery process, privacy information and four substantive guides are also available.

The 16 original tool definitions were recovered from the previous Netlify build. Financial tools run locally using transparent scenario formulas, explicitly accounting for contribution margin, recurring costs, setup, review effort and missing denominators. The assessment has six stages, explicit unanswered/NA handling and optional device drafts. Website Grader prepares an official PageSpeed test link; SEO/AEO Review inspects pasted HTML and does not claim to crawl a live site.

The former blog feed contained only a test post. Four practical guides replace that placeholder; no client results or testimonials were invented.

## Enquiry form

About, Contact and consultation pages include required name, email, phone and consent, plus optional service, solution, industry and message. Related contact links preselect a matching solution. After validation the visitor reviews a prepared message and chooses email or WhatsApp. The visitor sends from that app. Reviewing the form does not send or store a lead in a website inbox. Original email, phone, WhatsApp and office details were restored. The previous site's private appointment backend was not copied or connected; no false submission confirmation is shown.

## Video interaction

All four solution heroes initially show the existing poster with legible headline, introduction and a prominent central Play button. Clicking moves the text above the full-width player and starts playback. Custom five-second backward/forward controls accompany native volume, captions and fullscreen controls. Skips clamp to the media boundaries and preserve pause state. Playback pauses when the document becomes hidden. The transition respects reduced motion. Cached metadata is synchronised so skip controls work after hydration. Homepage animation assets and the original GrowthOS media are preserved.

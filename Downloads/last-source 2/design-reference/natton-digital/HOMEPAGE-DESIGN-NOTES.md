# Homepage redesign — manager review, 4 November 2026

## Implemented scope

Only the homepage body and homepage closing panel were redesigned. The shared header, footer link grid, routes and original hero source/assets remain intact. Detail-page content and approved service/product/industry designs are unchanged.

- The approved colour mockup's aqua blue (#77d5dd) is applied only to Why choose us; every other section background is restored. Matte charcoal (#111a20) headings and dark body copy (#202d35) strengthen the reading hierarchy; warm white reason cards separate detailed work samples from the blue base.
- Who we are retains its prior white background and photograph treatment. Text across the homepage is darker, including tables, labels and simulated interface artwork; light text remains on dark surfaces for contrast. The hero, shared navigation and dark closing panel retain their previous treatments; footer copy is darker without changing its background. Forest, copper and cyan destination accents remain tied to offering links.
- Who we are retains the approved two paragraphs and the existing team scene, with a readable solid caption panel.
- Why choose us presents larger, darker work examples immediately after each title and before its description, with a heavier section heading. It retains all six approved headings/descriptions and replaces symbolic graphics with specific examples: delivery board, campaign brief, enquiry table, sample campaign review, customer conversation and support queue.
- Services and product overview cards have a full-width scene/workspace layout. The service list retains all 13 routes, products all eight routes, and solutions all four. The agent card remains focused on three relevant products.
- The CRM example has fictitious contacts, stages, ownership and a next action. Its button moves Meera Shah into In conversation and announces the change. A labelled contact record fills the adjacent content column meaningfully.
- How we work retains its existing copy and keyboard interactions. Each stage shows a concrete example brief, scope, launch checklist or review note rather than three abstract boxes.
- Two stacked outcome marquees stay after How we work. Their bands are compact and saturated, with clear text. There is no intersecting-ribbon artwork.
- Tools remain separate, with names and authentic brand logos and no links. Compact wrapping replaces oversized sparse tiles.
- Industry photography, all eight supplied clientele logos, the exact Our Clientele heading and three FAQs remain present. FAQs use the full content width rather than an unbalanced column.
- The homepage closing panel has a stronger teal background, readable white copy and a clear consultation action.

## Visual assets

One new AI-generated illustrative office photograph shows a sales operations team reviewing a CRM on a silver laptop. It is not presented as an actual Natton team or client. Its prompt/provenance is recorded in public/media/homepage/crm-photo-source.json. Accurate example interface panels are native HTML, not generated text. Both image widths are compressed WebP files; existing body photographs remain lazy loaded.

## Functional limits

All new records, people names inside UI, project scopes and metrics are explicitly illustrative. No real client results or certifications are claimed. Buttons update local display state only. No CRM integration, message sending, live agent or audio calling was added.

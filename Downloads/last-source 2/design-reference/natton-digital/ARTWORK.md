# Reference-derived material artwork

These assets were edited with the built-in image generation tool using the exact user-selected reference image. No CLI image-generation fallback was used. The finished PNGs were encoded as WebP for the live website without cropping or changing their composition.

## Assets used by the pages

- `public/media/materials/sandstone-copper-v1.webp` — all product pages and product/overall overview backgrounds, 1536 × 1024.
- `public/media/materials/ivory-sage-v1.webp` — all industry pages and industry overview backgrounds, 1536 × 1024.

They are decorative backgrounds. All page text, controls, glass cards and moving SVG fields remain live HTML/CSS/React content. They are not baked into a screenshot. The original GrowthOS video is unchanged.

## Shared generation prompt

Use case: precise-object-edit. Input image is the EXACT edit target. Create a production website BACKGROUND MATERIAL PLATE by removing all interface content from this exact reference. Remove every card, rounded panel/frame, all text, icons, charts and labels; reconstruct the uncovered background in the SAME material language and lighting. No text, no logos, no UI, no boxes, no rectangular frames. This asset will sit behind live HTML glass cards, so it must be a continuous empty material backdrop, with plenty of open calm area for readable live content. Premium physically rendered surface, creamy translucent material, soft refracted sunlight, fluted architectural glass, clear metallic wire highlights with adjacent dark contact edge, fine natural grain. Landscape 1536 by 1024 composition.

## Copper prompt suffix

Keep and expand ONLY the reference's LEFT warm sandstone/champagne/copper background into the whole landscape canvas. Invariants: the reference's exact warm beige colour grading, vertical translucent fluting along the left edge, bright champagne light, the thin polished copper wire curving from the top centre toward the upper left, and another slim copper arc sweeping along the bottom edge. The copper wires pass behind where the future page panels will be, with specular shine and tiny grounded shadow. Soft architectural light streaks, natural warm highlights, material depth and luminous reflections like the source. The middle 70% stays calm ivory/sandstone with no objects. Do not include green, do not include any of the original text/cards.

## Sage prompt suffix

Keep and expand ONLY the reference's RIGHT ivory/sage/evergreen background into the whole landscape canvas. Invariants: warm ivory and muted sage grading, gentle dappled foliage shadows and diagonal sunbeams across the top right, soft fluted glass at the far right edge, slim polished sage/olive metallic wires sweeping in broad organic arcs from upper right and around the bottom edges. The slim wire has bright ivory specular shine and a very narrow dark green contact edge, passing behind where future live panels will be. Beautiful translucent shade transitions, material depth and luminous reflections exactly like the source. The middle 70% stays calm pale ivory/sage. No copper/orange, no cards/text.

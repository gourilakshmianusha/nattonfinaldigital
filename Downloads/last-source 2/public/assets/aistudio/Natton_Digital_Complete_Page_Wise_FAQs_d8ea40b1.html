Q01What does Natton Digital actually do beyond regular digital marketing?

Natton Digital brings marketing and business operations together. Alongside SEO, landing pages and Google or Meta campaigns, its solutions cover lead tracking, customer follow-ups, WhatsApp conversations and AI-powered workflows. The aim is not simply to bring people to your business, but to help your team respond to enquiries and turn more of the right ones into customers.

Q02Is Natton Digital suitable for a small business, or is it only for large companies?

Natton Digital positions its solutions for MSMEs in India and small to medium-sized businesses worldwide. A business can start by solving one important problem, such as missed enquiries or slow follow-ups, rather than implementing every tool at once. The right scope depends on your goals, current systems and budget.

Q03How do we decide which of Natton Digital’s five solutions we need first?

Start with the biggest gap in your customer journey. If too few people discover you, look at AI Growth Marketing. If creating campaigns takes too long, explore AI Marketing SaaS. If leads are scattered, consider CRM & Automation SaaS. If responses are slow, look at Conversational AI. For repetitive back-office work, explore AI Agents & Workflows.

S01Which services can I choose from at Natton Digital?

The services menu covers website and e-commerce development, SEO, Google Business Profile, paid advertising, social media, content, graphic design, email automation, AEO and AI search optimization, analytics, conversion optimization, and AI Growth Marketing. S02What is the difference between an individual service and an integrated solution?

An individual service addresses a specific need, such as improving a website or managing Google Ads. An integrated solution connects several services and tools around an outcome, such as generating enquiries and following them up through a CRM. The right choice depends on what already works and where customers are being lost.

S03Can I start with one service and add others later?

A phased approach lets you address the most urgent problem first and expand when the results, budget, and team capacity justify it. For example, a business might improve a landing page before increasing advertising spend. Agree on priorities, account access, and reporting at the beginning so later work can build on the same foundation.

Website Design & Development

4 FAQs

Q07Do I need to replace my existing website before using these services?

Not necessarily. If your current website works well, the first step may be to improve its content, landing pages, enquiry forms or connections to your CRM. A redesign might only be needed if the website is slow, difficult to use or not converting visitors. The choice should follow a review of your present site and business goals.

S04What should a business website design and development project include?

A useful scope can cover page structure, mobile layouts, clear service information, enquiry forms, navigation, and testing before launch. Content writing, booking tools, CRM connections, hosting, and ongoing maintenance should be listed separately where needed. Agree on the pages, features, responsibilities, and acceptance checks before development begins.

S05What information do I need to provide before the website project starts?

Prepare your brand assets, service details, target audience, preferred examples, contact information, and any existing website access. It also helps to explain the action visitors should take, such as requesting a quote or booking a call. Missing content, approvals, or access can affect the delivery schedule, so identify these early.

S06Will I receive access to manage my website after launch?

Website access, editable content, training, and handover should be agreed in the project scope. Ask which changes you can make yourself and which need technical support. Domain ownership, hosting, backups, third-party licences, maintenance responsibilities, and any ongoing fees should also be recorded so you understand how the site will be managed.

Search Engine Optimization (SEO)

3 FAQs

S07What does SEO work involve for a business website?

SEO work can include understanding customer searches, improving page content and structure, fixing technical issues, strengthening internal links, and making useful pages easier to discover. The priorities should come from an audit and your business goals. A local service business and a large online store will usually need different plans.

S08How long does it take to see results from SEO?

SEO results develop over time and depend on your website's starting point, competition, content quality, and the work completed. Some fixes may be noticed sooner than broader ranking improvements. Agree on review milestones and track relevant search visibility, website visits, and qualified enquiries instead of expecting a fixed result by a guaranteed date.

S09Can an SEO service guarantee the number one position on Google?

No provider controls Google's search results, so a specific ranking should not be guaranteed. A useful SEO plan explains the work, the reasons for each priority, and the measures used to review progress. Focus on searches that matter to your customers and the enquiries or sales those visits support.

Google Business Profile (GMB/GBP)

3 FAQs

S10Can this service help with a new or existing Google Business Profile?

A profile review can identify missing or inaccurate business details, unsuitable categories, outdated hours, and opportunities to improve photos and descriptions. If a profile already exists, check ownership and access before creating another listing. Verification and account decisions remain with Google, and the profile must accurately represent the business.

S11What can improve my business's visibility on Google Maps?

Keep business details accurate and complete, choose appropriate categories, add relevant photos, and respond helpfully to genuine reviews. Google describes local ranking in terms of relevance, distance, and prominence. A service can improve the information you provide, but it cannot buy a better organic local position or guarantee a Maps ranking.

Google Business Profile guidance on local ranking

S12Do I still need a website if I have a Google Business Profile?

The two serve different purposes. Your profile helps people discover essential business information on Google Search and Maps. Your website gives you more room to explain services, show work, answer questions, and collect enquiries. Keeping names, contact details, services, and location information consistent helps customers move between them confidently.

PPC & Google Ads Management

3 FAQs

S13Is the Google Ads budget included in the management fee?

Advertising spend and campaign management are different costs and should be shown separately in the proposal. The scope should also explain any landing-page, creative, tracking, or software charges. There is no single suitable budget for every business; it depends on your audience, location, competition, and the outcome you want to measure.

S14How do we know whether Google Ads are bringing useful leads?

Define a useful enquiry before launching and connect the appropriate form, call, booking, or purchase events to your reporting. Where the setup allows it, review what happens after the enquiry reaches the sales team. Assess lead quality and customer outcomes alongside clicks and cost per lead, rather than treating traffic alone as success.

S15Should I choose Google Ads, SEO, or both?

Google Ads can give you paid visibility while campaigns are active, while SEO builds the usefulness and discoverability of your website over time. The choice depends on urgency, competition, budget, and your website's readiness to convert visitors. They can complement each other when the targeting, landing pages, and follow-up process support the same goal.

Social Media Marketing

3 FAQs

S16Which social media platforms should my business focus on?

Choose platforms around your customers, the content you can produce consistently, and the action you want people to take. A visually led consumer brand may need a different approach from a business selling professional services. It is usually more useful to manage a few relevant channels well than to post everywhere without a clear purpose.

S17Can social media content be planned and approved before it is published?

A content calendar and approval process can give your team visibility over themes, captions, artwork, and posting dates before publication. Agree on who supplies information, who approves content, and how much time is needed for feedback. Posting frequency, video production, community replies, and revision limits should be defined in the scope.

S18How is social media marketing success measured?

Match the measures to the objective. Awareness work may focus on relevant reach and engagement; an enquiry campaign should also track website visits, messages, bookings, or qualified leads. Follower growth alone does not show whether the right people are becoming customers. Review the quality of responses and the next steps people take.

Meta Ads (Facebook & Instagram)

3 FAQs

S19How are Meta Ads different from regular Facebook and Instagram posts?

Regular posts support your ongoing presence and community. Meta Ads use a paid budget to distribute campaign content toward selected objectives and audiences. They can support discovery, enquiries, or sales, but results depend on factors such as the offer, creative, audience, landing page, and follow-up process. Advertising spend is separate from organic posting work.

S20Can one campaign use both Facebook and Instagram?

A campaign may use placements across Facebook and Instagram when the selected objective, account setup, creative, and available placements support it. The platform mix should follow the audience and results, rather than an assumption that every placement will work equally well. The campaign plan should explain which channels and formats will be tested.

S21Do I need new creative and a landing page before running Meta Ads?

You need a clear offer and suitable creative, and the destination must match what the ad promises. Depending on the campaign, that destination might be a website page, an enquiry form, or a messaging conversation. Existing assets can be reviewed first; new design, copy, or development should be included explicitly if required.

Content Marketing & Blogging

3 FAQs

S22How are blog topics chosen for my business?

Useful topics come from customer questions, search needs, buying decisions, and gaps in your current website content. A plan should connect each topic to a relevant audience and business goal. Prioritize helpful explanations and original knowledge rather than publishing large amounts of content that repeat what visitors can already find elsewhere.

S23Can AI be used to create content without losing my brand's voice?

AI can assist with research outlines, early drafts, and content variations, while people check accuracy, tone, originality, and suitability for the audience. Your brand guidance and subject knowledge should shape the final result. Facts, examples, sensitive statements, and product or service claims need review before anything is published.

S24How do we measure whether blogs are helping the business?

Look at relevant search visits, engagement with useful pages, clicks to services, and enquiries or purchases associated with the content. A blog may also help prospects understand an offer before contacting your team. Review content over an appropriate period and update useful pages when information changes instead of judging performance by article count alone.

AEO & AI Search Optimization

3 FAQs

S25What is AEO, and how does it relate to SEO?

Answer engine optimization focuses on making useful information easy to understand and answer from, including in AI-assisted search experiences. It overlaps with SEO through clear content, accessible pages, internal links, and reliable information. For Google's AI search features, established SEO fundamentals still apply; AEO is not a substitute for them.

Google Search Central guidance on AI search features

S26Can AEO guarantee that my business appears in AI answers?

No. AI search providers decide which information to use and display, and those choices can change between questions and experiences. The work should improve the quality, clarity, and accessibility of your content without promising a particular mention or citation. For Google, meeting eligibility requirements does not guarantee inclusion in an AI answer.

Google Search Central guidance on AI search features

S27Do I need special AI files or a completely new website for AEO?

Not necessarily. Start by improving how clearly your existing pages explain your business, services, and customer questions. For Google's AI features, there is no special AI file or unique structured-data type that must be added. Other platforms may have different guidance, so prioritize useful content and check platform-specific requirements before adding extra technical work.

Email Marketing & Automation

3 FAQs

S31Which customer journeys can email automation support?

Email workflows can support approved journeys such as welcoming subscribers, responding to enquiries, nurturing interested prospects, and sending relevant customer updates. E-commerce journeys may also include appropriate post-purchase or cart-related messages. Analytics & Conversion Rate Optimization

3 FAQs

S37What is the difference between analytics and conversion rate optimization?

Analytics helps explain how visitors find and use your website. Conversion rate optimization uses that evidence, customer research, and testing to improve the journey toward a useful action, such as an enquiry or purchase. Together they help you identify where people drop off and prioritize improvements with a business purpose.

S38Can existing website and advertising data be used?

Existing data can be useful after checking that the setup measures the right actions consistently. Review forms, bookings, purchases, campaign tags, and reporting definitions before drawing conclusions. Missing events, duplicate tracking, or changes in consent settings can affect the picture. Establish a dependable baseline before comparing performance or recommending major changes.

S39Does conversion optimization mean increasing website traffic?

Its main focus is helping a greater share of relevant visitors complete the intended action. Improvements may involve page clarity, enquiry forms, trust information, navigation, or checkout steps. More traffic can help a business grow, but it does not automatically fix a weak conversion journey. Test changes against suitable measures without assuming every change will improve results.
